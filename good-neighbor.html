The Good Neighbor Center · Case study

Making community support easier to find.

The Good Neighbor Center supports children’s learning through community tutoring. Our team redesigned the website and explored a companion app, keeping its bright, youthful character while making services and ways to help easier to navigate.

01

The challenge

Parents needed nearby, affordable tutoring and enough information to decide whether the center was right for their children. Volunteers and donors needed equally clear routes to contribute. The outdated site needed to serve these different audiences without losing the organization’s character.

02

My contribution

I was responsible for my portion of user research and testing, along with concepts and the redesign of the volunteering function across the website and app. The project was collaborative, with the broader experience developed as a team.

03

Listen to the community

The team interviewed 10 people with different ages and backgrounds. Proximity and access were recurring concerns. Findings also emphasized quick donations, transparency about how contributions are used, and detailed information before committing to tutoring.

04

Design for several kinds of support

Three personas helped the team consider parents, volunteers, and donors throughout development. The value proposition centered on learning about services, discovering volunteer opportunities, and supporting free tutoring through donations.

05

A structure people can follow

The sitemap organized the center’s story, services, people, volunteering, and donations. Sketches and two to three wireframe iterations explored these paths. The homepage brought free tutoring and several donation options into a consistent desktop and mobile structure.

06

Keep the character, improve the experience

The final Figma direction retained the original’s vibrant colors and playful feeling. A filing-tab treatment on the active page created a recognizable navigation cue. The key lesson was to make services, volunteering, and donations clear for every audience.
