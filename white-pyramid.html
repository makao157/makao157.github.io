White Pyramid Clothing · Case study

A shopping experience with a point of view.

White Pyramid needed a digital experience that reflected the people wearing its clothing: artists, travelers, and board athletes who see style as self-expression. Our team explored the shopping journey from research through high-fidelity design.

01

The challenge

The existing shopping experience was out of step with the brand’s younger, style-driven audience. The redesign aimed to strengthen the brand’s position, simplify browsing and purchasing, and bring consistency to its website and mobile experience.

02

My contribution

As UX Designer and Scrum Master, I collaborated with another UX designer, a content strategist, a software engineer, and a project manager. The work brought together research, flows, wireframes, interface design, and development collaboration.

03

Research shaped the structure

Discovery interviews helped frame audience needs. Card sorting organized pain points, wants, and content requirements; competitive analysis grounded the direction in fashion-commerce conventions. The synthesis informed the information architecture and stakeholder discussions.

04

Iterate before polishing

Sketches and low-fidelity wireframes allowed the team to explore layout and navigation quickly. Prototypes were refined through stakeholder feedback and usability testing before moving to a high-fidelity Figma design for handoff.

05

A consistent visual language

The interface pairs dark and light natural hues with a bold fashion identity. Mobile patterns considered iOS guidelines, while regular developer reviews helped keep the design technically feasible.

06

Takeaway

The strongest part of the work was connecting brand identity with a usable shopping experience. A clear visual system and a coherent content structure helped the team carry the same brand position across screens.
