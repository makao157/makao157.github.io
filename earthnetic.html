Earthnetic · Case study

Turn environmental intention into local action.

People want to make more sustainable choices, but cost and inconvenience can get in the way. Earthnetic brings environmental event discovery and planning into one mobile concept, helping organizers and volunteers find practical ways to participate locally.

The challenge

Environmental events were often promoted through general-purpose platforms such as Facebook Events. Finding relevant activities nearby took effort. The opportunity was a focused experience for discovering and organizing community environmental events.

02

Research before assumptions

A preliminary survey of 50 participants explored recycling habits, environmental priorities, and willingness to join local events. Six interviews then explored sustainable-lifestyle barriers and previous event experiences. Cost and inconvenience emerged as key obstacles.

03

Make participation practical

An affinity diagram narrowed the direction to convenient, local, accessible, and affordable activities. Competitive analysis and feature prioritization helped the team decide what mattered most in the initial experience.

04

Choose the core task

Event planning became the leading feature. A friend feed initially seemed useful, but testing indicated it was a lower priority, so the team deprioritized it. This kept the experience focused on finding and participating in events.

05

From story to prototype

Three personas and a storyboard grounded the design in a user journey. Sketches became a low-fidelity prototype, which provided a structure for feedback and the final high-fidelity direction.

06

Takeaway

A useful environmental product needs to reduce the effort of taking action. The project resulted in a high-fidelity prototype, with local relevance and a focused task structure at the center of the design.
